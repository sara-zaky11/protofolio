A fashion experience designed around discovery

A fashion discovery platform that brings local Egyptian brands together in one curated space, helping users discover new brands, explore their products, and connect with the local fashion scene. The experience focuses on discovery, inspiration, and community rather than traditional online shopping.

Case study reflection

Problem

The local fashion scene offers many unique Egyptian brands, but discovering them can feel scattered and scattered across platforms. I wanted to create a more curated and engaging experience that makes it easier for users to discover local brands, explore their products, and find inspiration in one place.

Solution

I designed a curated space that brings local Egyptian brands together in one place. The experience helps users discover brands, explore products, get inspired, and connect with the local fashion scene through a simple and engaging interface.

What I learned

This project taught me that fashion design is not only about making an interface look attractive. It's about balancing brand personality, user needs, information, and usability. It continued the process: more user testing, personalization, accessibility, and the complete shopping-to-checkout experience.
